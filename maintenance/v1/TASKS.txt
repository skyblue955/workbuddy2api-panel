Design decisions
- Preserve messages:[] for legacy routing compatibility; reject missing/null/scalar messages. Do not declare complete OpenAI/Responses/Anthropic support.
- Bound inbound body64MiB to prevent unbounded allocation; supported extension fields remain untouched. Large-body behavior is a documented change.
- OAuth credentials are not legacy WorkBuddy client credentials; no automatic account transfer.
- Official limitations remain observable; no fabricated task progress, identity spoofing or bypass changes.
- Activity catalog verification date is a source-audit date, not live account eligibility. Referral rules are informational only; no new free-credit entitlement invented.
