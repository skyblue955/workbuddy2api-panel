## Classification rules

* **Hybrid** means the implementation performs a real operation (or a real upstream conversation/lookup) and also emits task telemetry. It still requires the genuine condition/effect to count.
* **Event-only** means the implementation emits telemetry or a synthetic event sequence without performing the product operation described by the task. The 17 event-only paths are not genuine completion; do not execute or repair them to obtain unearned rewards, and do not test which fake events work.
* “Real fulfillment shown” records what the source/tests establish, not what a telemetry response might imply. The matrix remains a static source inventory; the two rows below also record the bounded live validation evidence from 2026-11-07. Live evidence does not turn event-only paths into real completion.
* Side effects are inferred only from the source implementation. `0` means no product side effect is shown by the implementation; counts are event/action families, not reward amounts.

## Count and release-note caveats

* Exact inventory count: **25** (`autoActions` entries in source).
* **8 hybrids:** `first_buddy`, `Model_chat_GLM5.2`, `expert_5`, `Expert_team_use_3`, `Hp_Appearance`, `skill_1`, `Expert_lighthouse`, `black_cat`.
* **17 event-only:** all remaining rows. These are not true completion paths and must not be recommended for execution/fixing to obtain rewards or for probing which fabricated events are accepted.
* Real-action rows require bounded approval for any fee, terms/agreement, adoption, preference/theme mutation, expert/connector use, or other account effect. Do not blanket-run `auto_all`; do not reuse a prior approval.
* `skill_1` lacks proof of an actual skill invocation/loading. `Expert_lighthouse` lacks connector authorization/use proof. `automation_1`, `template_5`, and `create_canvas` emit events but do not perform creation. `Library_read` has only the historical Nov-4 genuine-user-reading/once-claim evidence noted above.
* Preserve legacy calls and wrappers; do not silently delete them. Simulations and mock passes are not live acceptance. No private JSON/credential reads or live calls were used for this inventory.
